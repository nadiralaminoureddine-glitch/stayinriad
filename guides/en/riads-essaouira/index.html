Riads in Essaouira

Ramparts facing the Atlantic, a white medina with blue shutters listed by UNESCO, a busy fishing port: Essaouira is Morocco's most relaxed city, and its riads often have a rooftop with an ocean view.

Which area should you choose?

Around Moulay Hassan squareBetween the port and the medina: cafés, fish restaurants, everything within walking distance.
The SkalaAlong the ramparts and cannons: riads with ocean views and sunsets.
The MellahIn the north of the medina, more authentic and often more affordable.
Near Bab Sbaa and Bab DoukkalaNear the gates: handy for the beach, parking and the bus station.

When to go

The climate is mild all year round and rarely very hot. The trade wind blows hard, especially in spring and summer: great for kitesurfing, less so for sunbathing. Bring a jumper for the evenings.

Our tips

  • Ask for a room or terrace with an ocean view.
  • The medina is small: everything is within walking distance.
  • It is often windy: a sheltered terrace is a real plus.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I stay in Essaouira?

In the medina, near Moulay Hassan square to walk everywhere, or along the Skala for the ocean view.

Is Essaouira hot in summer?

Rarely: the ocean and the wind keep it cool. It is a good stop when Marrakech is very hot.

How far is Essaouira from Marrakech?

About 3 hours by road or bus.
