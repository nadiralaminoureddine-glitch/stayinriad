Riads in Chefchaouen

Tucked into the Rif mountains, Chefchaouen is famous for its blue-painted lanes. Its guesthouses, often called 'dar', are smaller than in Marrakech, with terraces looking out over the mountains.

Which area should you choose?

Outa el Hammam squareThe heart of the medina, facing the kasbah: lively, close to the restaurants.
Ras el MaAt the top of the medina, near the spring and wash house: quieter, with mountain views.
Bab el AinThe main gate of the medina: handy when arriving with luggage.

When to go

Spring and autumn are ideal. Summer is pleasant in the mountains. Winter is cold and rainy: choose a heated room.

Our tips

  • The medina is on a slope: ask for a riad near Bab el Ain if you have suitcases.
  • Walk up to the Spanish Mosque at sunset for the view over the blue city.
  • Book early for weekends and holidays: the town is small.

Frequently asked questions

Which area should I stay in in Chefchaouen?

Near Outa el Hammam square to be central, or towards Ras el Ma for peace and views.

How do I get to Chefchaouen?

By bus or taxi from Tangier, Tetouan or Fez. There is no train station or airport in town.

When should I visit Chefchaouen?

Spring and autumn. Winter is cold and rainy in the Rif.
